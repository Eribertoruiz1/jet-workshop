## Step 0 (gate 2)

### Source checked against the brief

The PDF (`.cache/`, never committed) confirms the research summary:

- Three wings on one body, Langley 8-foot transonic tunnel. Aspect ratio 3.5, taper ratio 0.2, 4 percent thick streamwise, zero twist, incidence and dihedral.
- Quarter-chord sweep 10.8, 35 and 47 deg. Mach 0.50 to about 1.12 (figure 6(b) labels go to 1.13). Angle of attack -2 to 12 deg, limited at high subsonic and low supersonic Mach by the balance pitching-moment load. Reynolds number 2.0 to 2.5 million on the mean aerodynamic chord.
- Declared accuracy (report p. 5): CL ±0.01, CD ±0.001, Cm ±0.004.

Refinements and facts the brief did not state:

- Airfoil: NACA 65A thickness distribution; mean line = 1/3 of NACA 230 series + NACA 6-series uniform-load mean line (a = 1.0) for cli = 0.1.
- Reference geometry (figure 3): wing area 1.143 sq ft (trapezoid carried to the centreline; b²/A = 24²/3.5 in² checks), span 24.00 in, root chord 11.430 in, tip chord 2.286 in, mean aerodynamic chord 7.874 in at 4.667 in from the centreline. Body: cylinder 2.25 in in diameter with an ogive nose (20 in radius, 6.61 in long), 25.15 in long; body diameter / span = 0.094.
- Corrections: no blockage or wake-buoyancy corrections (slotted throat); corrected for stream misalignment; drag corrected to body base pressure equal to free-stream static pressure. Reflected shocks: no data between M 1.04 and 1.08; crossplots are dashed there.
- Body-alone data are not in this report; they are in its reference 4 (relevant to gate 4).
- Figure 9 (CD0 against M) is a crossplot: faired curves without symbols. The measured points are the symbols of the polars, figures 5(b), 6(b), 7(b). Each symbol carries a centre dot.
- The NTRS download has a 436-byte wrapper before `%PDF-1.6`; the digitizer README gives the strip command and both SHA-256 values.

### Digitizer

`tools/digitize/` (Python, pinned in `requirements.txt`). Pixel coordinates refer to each page's embedded scan at native resolution (about 300 dpi). The scans are skewed (about 0.5 to 0.9 deg, with shear) and warped (affine residuals up to 0.00035 in CD on figure 6(b)). The mesh calibration traces every grid line (fit RMS about 0.3 px) and interpolates inside each grid cell; residuals at the picked intersections are below 0.00006 in CD.
