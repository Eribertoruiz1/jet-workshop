## Gate 1: geometry sheet

File: `docs/studies/heldout-geometry.md`, alone on branch `lane/heldout-geometry` (= `origin/main` + one commit, `7c361e0`). Sources read: the text layers of both reports, then every value checked on the native scans (about 300 dpi): RM L51I04a figures 3 and 5, Table I, the text of pp. 4 to 7; RM L51K16a figures 1, 2 and 6 (captions), Table I, the text of pp. 2 to 6.

Contents: six configurations with IDs, planform, body and wing position, airfoil definition and the full Table I coordinates, test conditions side by side (tunnel, Mach numbers as labelled on each figure, gap, Reynolds number, stagnation conditions, angle range, declared accuracy, balance and support, axes), corrections, boundary layer, model construction (quoted), the same-models question, and an index of what each figure plots.

Checks made on the sheet:

- Mechanical scan for result-like text: every number after a coefficient name is a declared accuracy (CL ±0.01 / ±0.005, CD ±0.001, Cm ±0.004 / ±0.001) or the mean-line design lift coefficient 0.1; the only comparative word is "lower" in "lower surface". Output of the scan:

  ```
  -- coefficient followed by a number:
  72:lift coefficient of 0.1
  123:CL ±0.01  123:CD ±0.001  123:Cm ±0.004  123:CL ±0.005  123:CD ±0.001  123:Cm ±0.001
  161:CL, one polar at M 1.60
  -- comparative or trend words:
  74, 76, 106, 107: lower   (all "lower surface")
  ```

- Table I: transcribed from RM L51K16a, then every value checked against the scans of both reports (they print the same values). The RM L51I04a text layer reads 5.166 at x/c 65 of the root station; both scans show 5.168.
- `computed` values in the sheet: the station of the quarter-chord point of the mean aerodynamic chord (16.61 in) and the apex stations of the 35 and 10.8 deg wings (10.49 and 12.86 in), from the printed dimensions; the root chord, tip chord, mean aerodynamic chord and its station recomputed from aspect ratio, taper ratio, span and area agree with the printed values to the third decimal.
- `read by eye`: the Reynolds number of RM L51I04a figure 5 at seven Mach numbers, once, about ±0.02 million.

For the PM's check (judgment calls on what counts as a result):

1. **Withheld.** RM L51I04a, PDF p. 8 (7), says that wing 2 (`47deg-t06`) had removable plates used for attaching nacelles, so its surface "may not have been entirely aerodynamically smooth". It is a model fact, but the report states it only inside the discussion of the drag results, as a possible explanation of them; putting it in the sheet would pass on a hint that the authors saw something on that wing. Left out of the sheet; the owner confirmed (decision 1 below).
2. **Included.** RM L51K16a, PDF p. 6 (5): the authors judged from a body-alone Reynolds-number sweep (their figure 4) that the body boundary layer was primarily turbulent above a body-length Reynolds number of 7 million, and ran every test at about 15 lb/sq in for that reason. It sets the test condition and gives no wing-body value, so it is in the sheet, without the body-alone values.
3. **Included.** The twelve Mach labels of `47deg-t04` in RM L51I04a figure 6 are the same as those of the 47 deg wing in the open RM L52B08 figure 7. That is a test condition. Whether the data are the same test is answered at gate 2 below, on this branch only.

Answer to the trap "¿Los mismos modelos?": neither report says the models are the same physical models; both descriptions are quoted in the sheet. They differ in wording (bodies built integrally with each wing; interchangeable wings with integral body sections) and in the drawn body length (25.15 against 25.12 in).

### RM L51K16a, figure 6(c), drag plot: step 0

- **Scan.** PDF p. 20 (printed 19), upright, greyscale (256 levels), 2354 x 3096 px. Of another kind than spike 1:
  - fine-ruled graph paper, with lines every 0.005 in CL and 0.002 in CD (about 7 px), medium lines every 0.025 and 0.01, heavy lines every 0.05 and 0.02;
  - plain circles of radius about 13 px with no centre dot;
  - grey levels: symbol ink 30 to 100, heavy lines 110 to 145, fine lines 170 to 210;
  - 1 px = 0.00070 in CL and 0.00028 in CD (RM L51I04a: 0.0016 and 0.00016).
- **New method: `snap: "ring"`** in `digitize.py`. It fits a circle to the dark outline pixels; the graph-paper lines are lighter and drop out. A coarse search over centres within 6 px of the pick and radii from 8 to 18 px is followed by a least-squares fit with outlier rejection. Robustness:
  - 25 picks spread over ±6 px around each of the 8 circles gave the same centre to 0.02 px, with no failure;
  - pass B reran the fit at ink levels 70, 100 and 130 and windows of 20 and 26 px, with centres within 0.3 px.
- **Calibration.**
  - Pass A, heavy lines only (21 x 11), 10 intersections: `affine rms residual x 0.00034, y 0.000086; 1 px = 0.00070 x, 0.000281 y`; `mesh: 32 lines traced; worst line fit rms 0.423 px; max |residual| at picked intersections x 0.00035, y 0.000206`.

- **Symbols.** 8 single circles in both passes (pass B also counted 8 on the moment and angle plots). Every ring fit: radius 12.8 to 13.4 px, 176 to 208 ring pixels, rms 0.84 to 1.01 px, 16 of 16 sectors.
