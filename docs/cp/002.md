### For decision at this gate

1. **Step 5, start of the rise for the 47° wing.** With the brief's definition (`sampledDragRiseMach` on the NACA curve, dCD/dM = 0.1), the 47° curve never gets that steep: its steepest stretch between samples is 0.096 (M 0.965 to 0.99). The 0.97 in the brief is the figure the report's text gives, and validation report section 4 already shows "not reached" for that wing. I did not write 0.97 by hand. The app picks the sentence by computation: if every wing has a value, the brief's sentence. If only the 47° wing has none (today's case), it says: "la subida empieza en Mach 0.92 y 0.95, y la curva de 47° sube tan despacio que nunca llega a dispararse". In any other case, a short version with no rise clause. Options: keep this wording; drop the rise clause; or show the report's 0.97 labelled as the report's stated value (a fifth kind of figure, which needs a rule).
2. **Step 6, 13 % against 11 %.** `measuredLiftSlope` at M 0.50 gives 0.0650 per degree for the 10.8° wing and 0.0566 for the 47° wing (validation report, section 3): 12.9 % less. The text will say 13 %. Nothing was tuned. It is worth the PM checking where 11 came from.
3. **"Círculos" in steps 2 and 4 against the shapes rule.** The art direction gives each wing its own shape (circle, square, triangle), but step 4's text says "mira los mismos círculos" while the 47° wing is drawn with triangles. I kept the script word for word. Possible fix: "mira las mismas medidas", or circles for every wing (then shape no longer tells the wings apart in grey).

### 2. Validation rule (decision 0001)

`packages/validation/src/score.ts`:

- `compare(band, D, u_D)` returns `E = S − D` (S the band centre), `u_input` (half-width), `u_val = sqrt(u_D² + u_input²)` and `|E| / u_val`; `withinUval` is `|E| ≤ u_val`.
- The strip is `validated` when every symbol the chart draws (`SYMBOL_WINDOW` = 0.06 of the selected CL, now exported from the validation package and used by the app) has `|E| ≤ u_val` and `u_input ≤ 0.0020` at each. The previous rule stays as `reliability(..., "spike-1")`, used only by the report's side-by-side count.
- `disagrees` (the screen's "disagrees by more than the published precedent") keeps its meaning: a symbol more than 0.0020 outside the band, and never on a validated segment.

Report: a new section 10, generated. 10a gives, for every symbol with |CL| ≤ 0.3 where the model is in range, E, u_D, u_input, u_val and |E| / u_val, with a per wing and Mach summary for both models. 10b shows once, side by side, the validated segments under both rules (korn-mason-lock band, three tested wings, selected CL −0.1 to 0.5 every 0.1, 13 segments each): **3 validated with the previous rule, 11 with decision 0001**, out of 20 and 18 segments that hold symbols under each rule.

Tests: `packages/validation/test/criterion.test.ts` (5 tests): E and u_val; a symbol just outside the band but within u_val validates under the new rule and not under the old; a wide band (u_input > 0.0020) never validates; a symbol outside the drawn window does not change the strip; the disagreement flag.

The bench's chart note now says "symbols within 0.06, drawn and scored by the strip".
